02 / A more useful record

Give every find a clear status.

Instead of collecting more links without context, record what you know and what remains unresolved.

A useful Litbuy product spreadsheet distinguishes an interesting item from one you have actually researched.

A / IDENTIFIED

Know the exact version

Save the listing URL, seller name, model identifier, selected color and size. Keep variant notes beside the link so similar photographs do not blur different products together.

B / QUESTION OPEN

Write down the missing detail

Use specific questions: “Is this chest measurement flat or around the body?” is more useful than “Check sizing.” Leave unknown information visibly unresolved.

C / EVIDENCE SAVED

Keep observations traceable

Record where each claim came from: a listing description, seller response or item photograph. A stock image shows design; it does not establish the condition of the item you will receive.

D / RECHECK NEEDED

Refresh before deciding

Add a last-checked date. Revisit availability, selected variants, quoted costs and purchase terms before ordering, especially when an entry has been sitting in your list.

Example research note

Black jacket · size M selected · shoulder measurement missing · composition stated by seller · shipping not quoted · status: awaiting information.

This is an illustrative record, not an available product or price quotation.

03 / Category-specific questions

Research for the way it fits.

Apply different checks to different categories. A jersey size label and a fitted jacket size label are not interchangeable measurements.

Everyday layers

For T-shirts, hoodies, sweaters and jackets, start with a garment you already wear comfortably.

  • Compare chest, length, shoulder and sleeve measurements.
  • Ask how the garment was measured.
  • Record composition and care instructions.
Browse T-Shirts ↗

Movement & proportions

For pants, shorts, sets and jerseys, research the relationship between the pieces and your intended fit.

  • Record waist, rise, inseam and relevant stretch information.
  • Check whether set pieces have separate measurements.
  • Ask about the intended jersey cut.
Browse Pants/Shorts ↗

Contact & finishing details

For headwear, underwear and accessories, consider comfort, materials and category-specific purchase terms.

  • Check fit range, dimensions and closures.
  • Ask about materials if you have sensitivities.
  • Review any hygiene-related return conditions.

International shopping notes

Keep price and delivered cost separate.

Record the currency, destination and quotation date. An unquoted charge should remain “unknown,” rather than silently becoming zero.

Item price + applicable delivery + service fees + shipping + taxes or duties Some charges may not apply. Final amounts depend on the seller, service, destination and shipping method.

04 / Straight answers

Before you use a product list.

Understand what a spreadsheet can organize—and which details still need evidence.

What is a Litbuy spreadsheet?

A Litbuy spreadsheet is a product-link research sheet associated with shopping through Litbuy. Its usefulness depends on the accuracy of its links, variants, measurements and supporting evidence. This page is an independent guide, not an official Litbuy spreadsheet or live inventory feed.

Can I download a spreadsheet from this page?

No downloadable spreadsheet is provided here. This page offers a category directory and a research framework. Its shopping links lead to CAFANS.

How should I handle a product link that no longer works?

Mark the entry as unavailable, keep its model and variant notes, and search for a replacement listing. Recheck the replacement independently because its seller, materials, sizing and terms may differ.

Do reference photographs confirm CAFANS availability or authenticity?

No. Reference photographs illustrate fashion categories. They do not establish CAFANS stock, seller authorization, authenticity, affiliation or product quality.

Can spreadsheet prices predict the delivered total?

A listed product price alone cannot predict the delivered total. Record the currency and check applicable domestic delivery, service fees, international shipping, taxes and duties for the destination.
